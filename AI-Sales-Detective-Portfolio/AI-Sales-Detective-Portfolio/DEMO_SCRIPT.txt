AI SALES DETECTIVE — DEMONSTRATION GUIDE

1. Begin in VS Code and explain the problem statement.
2. Show app.py, main.py, train_model.py, the data directory, and requirements.txt.
3. Run the web dashboard:
   python -m streamlit run app.py
4. Upload data/sales.csv from the sidebar.
5. Show the Sales Overview and Revenue Trend.
6. Compare earlier and recent records. Mention that the split may use different numbers of days.
7. Explain the regression estimate, selected features, and MAE.
8. Show the IQR-based unusual-sales detection.
9. Read the Sales Investigation Report and Root-Cause Analysis. Stress that proposed explanations are hypotheses.
10. Show View Sales Data and the download option.
11. Conclude with the libraries used and the main lesson learned.

Optional input-validation tests:
- data/bad_sales.csv
- data/bad_date.csv
- data/negative_sales.csv

Remember: forecasts are experimental estimates, outliers need human review, and observed changes do not prove root causes.
